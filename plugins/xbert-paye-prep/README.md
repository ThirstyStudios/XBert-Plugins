# XBert PAYE Prep (UK)

PAYE, payroll and pension control account review for UK clients. Reconciles to HMRC RTI submissions, verifies auto-enrolment compliance, and produces a Word audit document.

## What it does
- Reconciles PAYE control account to RTI submissions (FPS / EPS)
- Reconciles payroll control to gross pay from the payroll reports
- Verifies pension liability matches employer plus employee contributions at the correct auto-enrolment rates (3% / 8%)
- Surfaces aged balances over three months with the period they relate to
- Confirms NIC categories, student loan plans and CIS deductions where applicable
- Produces a Word audit document with the supporting evidence

## Prerequisites
- XBert account
- Client connected to a UK-domiciled ledger
- Payroll posted for the period with RTI submissions made via PAYE-compatible software

## Usage
After installing, type:

    /paye-prep

in any Claude chat (Desktop or Code).
