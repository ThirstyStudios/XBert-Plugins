# XBert MTD Prep (UK)

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax readiness assessment for UK clients. Determines whether the bookkeeping is mapped, classified and structured for MTD quarterly reporting to HMRC.

## What it does
- Audits chart of accounts against HMRC SA103 (self-employment) and SA105 (property) categories
- Determines qualifying income threshold tier and mandatory MTD start date
- Checks simplified vs full reporting eligibility (under £90K)
- Ensures self-employment and property income are separated
- Splits residential vs commercial finance costs
- Produces a Word working paper with mapping audit, threshold determination, and required actions

## Prerequisites
- XBert account
- Client connected to a UK-domiciled ledger
- Client has self-employment or property income (qualifying income for MTD ITSA)

## Usage
After installing, type:

    /mtd-prep

in any Claude chat (Desktop or Code).
