# XBert Month-End Pack

Month-end financial pack the partner can hand to the client. Same shape every month — P&L vs budget, BS movement walk, cashflow vs forecast, and KPI roll-up in a single Word document.

## What it does
- Pulls P&L, balance sheet, cashflow, and budget adherence in one workflow
- Runs the balance sheet movement walk to flag unexpected account moves
- Calculates the standard KPI roll-up (gross margin, net margin, debtor days, creditor days, working capital)
- Assembles a Word document with consistent section order, named variances, and scaffolded commentary
- Surfaces a chat-side summary of top variances and partner-review focus areas

## Prerequisites
- XBert account
- Client ledger with at least one closed accounting period
- Budget loaded in the ledger (pack still runs without; budget sections degrade gracefully)

## Usage
After installing, type:

    /month-end-pack

in any Claude chat (Desktop or Code).
