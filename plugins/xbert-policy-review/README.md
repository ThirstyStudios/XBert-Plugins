# XBert Policy Review

Weekly review of posted spend against firm and client policy XBerts.

## What it does
- Resolves the period's linked policy XBert firings (out-of-policy categories, banned vendors, mileage caps, duplicate submissions, missing receipts, receipt forensic flags)
- Groups violations by policy category, with spender sub-grouping
- Drafts per-violation spender follow-ups for reviewer approval (never auto-sent)
- Produces an audit pack with hyperlinks back to every source XBert and captured receipt

## Prerequisites
- XBert account
- Client connected to a ledger
- Policy XBerts linked to the Policy Review agent in the Connect portal

## Usage
After installing, type:

    /policy-review

in any Claude chat (Desktop or Code).
