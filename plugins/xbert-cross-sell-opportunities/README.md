# XBert Cross-Sell Opportunities

Find your highest-value cross-sell opportunities by matching client profile to service uptake patterns inside your own book.

## What it does
- For every client, finds the top lookalike peers inside your Connect using XBert's similar-clients engine
- Identifies services the lookalike has that this client does not
- Scores and ranks each opportunity by lookalike prevalence, similarity score and inferred revenue uplift
- Caps the per-client list at three to keep it actionable
- Drafts talking points referencing the lookalike pattern
- Produces a Word document with per-client opportunity sheets and a portfolio-level summary

## Prerequisites
- XBert account
- XPM connected (for service uptake and client data)
- At least 20 clients in the Connect — lookalike matching needs a reasonable peer set

## Usage
After installing, type:

    /cross-sell-opportunities

in any Claude chat (Desktop or Code).
