# Phase 1 — Practice Owner / Practice Manager Workflow Catalogue

## Executive summary

This catalogue covers the recurring workflows performed by **practice owners and practice managers** at Australian/NZ accounting firms — distinct from bookkeeper or accountant work in that the unit of focus is the **book of clients, the team, and the practice P&L**, not a single client file. Workflows fall into five clusters:

1. **Capacity & utilisation** (1, 2, 3, 11) — already partly served by `xbert-capacity` and `xbert-workflow-review`.
2. **Pricing & profitability** (4, 13, 16, 19) — heavily influenced by the cowork #6 input: the industry is migrating off hourly to value/volume metrics, so fee-review and cost-to-serve jobs need to *expose* the variance between today's hourly basis and a volume-or-value alternative, not just report fees.
3. **Practice health & risk** (6, 7, 15) — cowork #2 input emphasised that the current descriptive health check needs a **diagnostic and prescriptive** layer; rows 6 and 7 are split accordingly (6 = descriptive roll-up across the book, 7 = diagnostic deep-dive on the Connect/XBert configuration that explains *why* health is what it is).
4. **Client lifecycle** (5, 9, 10, 20) — onboarding gate, engagement-letter cycles, churn signals, and cross-sell.
5. **Team & growth** (12, 17, 18) — workload rebalancing, offshore allocation, lead funnel, capability gaps.

Existing plugins (`xbert-capacity`, `xbert-pulse`, `xbert-workflow-review`, `xbert-reconcile`) are included for completeness and explicitly marked in `existing_plugin_match` so the Phase 2 auditor can dedupe. The seed list of 20 has been pruned to **18 rows** (lead funnel and service-line profitability merged into adjacent rows; cross-sell kept; staff training kept) and two diagnostic/prescriptive additions surfaced (rows 6a, 7a) per cowork inputs.

## Most surprising / additions beyond the seed list

1. **Row 6a — Practice health check, diagnostic & prescriptive layer (split from row 6).** The cowork #2 input is explicit that today's health check is descriptive only ("data quality is 72%") and the missing layer is "*why* and *what to change*". Splitting it out makes the gap auditable in Phase 2 rather than burying a critical-but-different capability inside row 6.

2. **Row 16 — Pricing model standardisation as a programme, not per-client.** Row 4 (per-client fee review) is the seed-list workflow. Row 16 is the **practice-wide migration plan** from hourly to volume/value — the cowork #6 input frames this as the industry shift, so the owner needs a sequenced programme view, not just a stack of per-client recommendations. The two rows do different jobs and should be catalogued separately.

3. **Row 12 — Outsourced / offshore work allocation review.** Not on the seed list but a standard ANZ practice-manager workflow given Philippines/India offshoring is now mainstream in mid-tier firms. Surfaced because the MCP tooling (`Features_GetUserCapacityAllocation` already differentiates users) makes it cheap to build — and because the existing `xbert-capacity` plugin treats the team as homogeneous, missing the offshore-vs-onshore cost/quality trade-off entirely.

## Coverage notes for Phase 2 auditor

- **Already-served by existing plugins:** rows 1 (xbert-capacity), 5 (xbert-pulse), 8 (xbert-workflow-review). Listed for dedup signal.
- **Partial overlap with existing plugins (worth flagging):** rows 2, 3, 11 sit adjacent to `xbert-capacity` but answer different owner-level questions — Phase 2 should decide whether to extend `xbert-capacity` or spin a sibling plugin.
- **Weakest MCP coverage:** row 17 (lead-to-client funnel) — current MCP inventory has no CRM tooling. Phase 2 needs a build/buy decision.
- **High-value adjacencies worth building together:** rows 4 + 10 + 13 + 16 form the "fee/profit cluster" and share most inputs; a single plugin scaffolding the four would beat four separate plugins.
- **PracticeAgent under-utilised:** only rows 6a and 7 route there; if Phase 2 finds more PracticeAgent surfaces, this catalogue should be revisited.
